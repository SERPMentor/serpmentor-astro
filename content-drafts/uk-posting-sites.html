Here is the thing about a list like this: 100 sites is about 90 more than you need.

You are not going to pitch all of them, and you should not try. The goal is not "get a link from every UK site with a high Domain Rating". The goal is a handful of relevant placements on sites your actual customers read.

This list is the raw material. Below the table, you will find how to filter it down to your shortlist, write a pitch that gets a reply, and avoid the mistakes that get you ignored or blocked.

How to pick your shortlist from this list

Work down the table in this order:

  1. Relevance. Cross out every site that has nothing to do with your industry or audience. For most businesses that removes 70 to 80 of the 100.
  2. Real traffic. Of what is left, keep the sites with meaningful monthly traffic. A link on a page nobody visits is a vanity link.
  3. Contribution policy. Check each remaining site for a "write for us", "contribute" or "editorial guidelines" page. Several of the national titles near the top only take PR pitches and expert commentary, not open guest posts.
  4. Reachability. Can you find a named editor or a clear submission route? A faceless contact form usually means a low reply rate.

You should end with 8 to 15 genuine targets. That is your list.

The pitch that gets a reply

Most guest-post pitches fail for the same reason. They lead with the ask instead of the idea.

Do this instead:

  1. Read three of their recent articles. Note the angle, the length and the tone.
  2. Find the gap. What have they not covered, or covered badly, that you can genuinely add to?
  3. Pitch the specific piece, not "I would like to write for you". Give the working headline, a two-line summary, and why their readers would care.
  4. Prove you can write. Link to one article you are proud of. One, not five.
  5. Keep it short. Four or five sentences. Editors skim.
  6. Follow up once, about a week later. Then move on.

The test: would this pitch still make sense if you removed the link you want entirely? If yes, send it. If the whole email only exists to get a backlink, the editor can tell.

What not to do

  • Do not send the same pitch to all 100 sites. Editors talk, and tools flag it.
  • Do not pay for a "guest post" that is really a link placement, without a sponsored tag. That is a paid link and it is a risk.
  • Do not stuff the post with exact-match anchor text pointing at your money pages.
  • Do not submit thin or AI-spun filler. A bad guest post damages your brand on someone else's site.
  • Do not chase Domain Rating at the expense of relevance. It is the number one mistake people make with lists like this.

What to realistically expect

Bottom line: five to ten relevant, editorial placements over a few months will do more for your rankings than fifty low-quality ones, and carry none of the risk. Use this list to find those five to ten.

Frequently asked questions

Are all 100 of these sites accepting guest posts?

No. Many of the national titles near the top take PR pitches and expert commentary, not open guest contributions. Check each site's editorial or "write for us" page before you pitch.

Do links from these sites have to be dofollow to be worth it?

A dofollow link passes more direct SEO value, but a nofollow link from a high-traffic, relevant UK site still drives referral traffic, builds brand awareness, and often leads to follow links elsewhere. Do not turn down a strong placement over the link attribute alone.

How many UK guest posts do I need to move my rankings?

Fewer than you would think. Five to ten relevant, editorial placements over a few months do more than fifty low-quality ones, and carry none of the risk.

Should I use a paid guest-posting service?

Be careful. Many sell placements on low-quality sites or undisclosed paid links. If you use one, insist on seeing the exact target sites in advance and check they have real traffic and genuine editorial standards.

The metrics in the table do not match what I see in my tools. Why?

Domain Rating, Domain Authority and traffic estimates are recalculated constantly and differ between tools. Treat the numbers here as a snapshot for sorting the list, and re-check the two or three sites you actually plan to pitch.
